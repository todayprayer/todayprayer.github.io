The psalm is one of the eighteen Psalms of Solomon. The first reading is from the wisdom and deuterocanonical books of the Douay–Rheims; the second from the pseudepigrapha — Enoch, Jubilees, Adam and Eve, Isaias, the Twelve Patriarchs, the Odes — from the Dead Sea scrolls, from the Apostolic Fathers, and from Dionysius the Areopagite on the Celestial Hierarchy.

For the morning, see Morning Prayer.
